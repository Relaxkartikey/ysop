## What Pro unlocks

- YSOP storage (connect your own Cloudflare R2 bucket)
- Permanent links
- Full storage management (quotas, multiple connected buckets)

## Pricing

Pro is billed in INR, monthly or yearly, through [Cashfree](https://www.cashfree.com). Pick a plan on the [checkout page](/checkout) to get started.

## Renewals & cancellation

Pro renews automatically at the end of each billing period. If you cancel, your Pro access stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for — cancelling stops the *next* renewal, it doesn't end your current period early.

## Payment issues

If a renewal payment fails, your account moves to a short grace period rather than losing Pro access immediately, giving the payment a chance to go through on retry. If it doesn't recover, the account reverts to Free once the grace period ends.
