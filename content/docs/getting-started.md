YSOP lets you upload a file, get a link back immediately, and choose when that link should stop working. No account is required to browse the site, but uploading needs a quick sign-in.

## Sign in

YSOP uses Google sign-in through Supabase. Click **Sign in** in the header and authorize with your Google account — that's the only sign-in method available.

## Upload your first file

1. Go to **Upload**.
2. Drag a file in, or click to browse.
3. Pick an expiry: 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days, or 10 days.
4. Upload. You'll get two links back — a **source link** (direct file URL) and a **share link** (a hosted download page).

That's it — the file is now live and will delete itself automatically once the expiry passes.
