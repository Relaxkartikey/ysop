## Expiry options

Every upload needs an expiry — this is the core of how YSOP works. Choose from:

- 24 hours
- 3 days
- 7 days
- 10 days

Once a file's expiry passes, it's deleted automatically — both the file itself and its listing in your dashboard. There's no way to recover it after that, so double-check the expiry before you upload something you need to keep around.

If you're on **Pro**, you can also mark a file as **permanent** instead of picking an expiry — it stays up until you delete it yourself.

## Multiple files & progress

You can select or drop several files at once. Each one uploads and shows its own progress bar, so a slow upload doesn't block the rest.

## What you get back

After a successful upload you get two links:

- **Source link** — points directly at the file in storage. Good for embedding in a webpage, a `<img>` tag, or any place that needs a direct file URL.
- **Share link** — a hosted page showing the filename, size, and a download button. Better for sending to someone who just wants to view and download the file.

## Managing uploads

Everything you upload shows up in your [dashboard](/dashboard), where you can rename files, move them between folders, copy either link again, or delete them early.
